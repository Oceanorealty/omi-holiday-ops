# Guest directory

`/operations/guests` provides an administrator-only index of existing guest records. Search names, email addresses, phone numbers or exact record IDs; filter for records linked to two or more confirmed non-block reservations. Results use 25-row pages. Counts include future reservations and exclude cancellations and inventory blocks, so they are not completed-stay or loyalty metrics.

Each record links to its own paginated reservation history. Matching names, emails and phone numbers do not merge separate guest IDs or imply that they are the same person. Contact edits remain in the reservation workflow. No marketing consent is inferred and no message is sent by this feature.

The filtered directory can be exported as a private, non-cacheable CSV, capped at 5000 records. Formula-like cells are escaped. Staff and anonymous requests cannot reach the index, profiles or export. No new tables or production data changes are required.

This is contact lookup and linked booking history, not a complete CRM: duplicate review/merge, explicit communication preferences, loyalty programmes and customer segmentation remain outstanding.
