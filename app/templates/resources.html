Shared resources & room-type inventory

Each reservation consumes the configured quantity from every resource attached to its listing. A physical room can have capacity 1; a pooled room type can have a larger capacity. Parent/child listing names alone do not connect inventory.

For an entire home and its separately sold rooms, attach the home to every physical-room resource and each room listing to its own resource. A block closes the full capacity of each attached resource. Existing reservations are preserved when mapping changes; capacity conflicts become visible for review.

This is local resource inventory. It does not assign a physical room to a pooled reservation or publish availability to an OTA. The iCal export includes fully unavailable dates for the next two years.
